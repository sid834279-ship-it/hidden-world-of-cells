Welcome to the World of Cells!

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. Every living organism is made of one or more cells. Each cell contains structures that perform special jobs.

Explore Plant and Animal Cells

Both plant and animal cells are eukaryotic cells. They contain a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.

🌿 Plant Cell

Special features: Cell wall, chloroplasts and a large central vacuole.

🐾 Animal Cell

Special features: No cell wall or chloroplasts. Its shape is often more flexible.

Meet the Cell Organelles

Organelles are specialised structures that perform different functions inside cells.

Nucleus
Contains DNA and helps regulate cell activities.
Mitochondria
Release usable energy through cellular respiration.
Ribosomes
Make proteins.
Cell Membrane
Controls what enters and leaves the cell.
Cytoplasm
Contains organelles and is where many reactions occur.
Cell Wall
Provides support and protection in plant cells.
Chloroplast
Uses light energy for photosynthesis in plant cells.
Vacuole
Stores water and other substances; the central vacuole helps support plant cells.
Endoplasmic Reticulum
Helps make and transport proteins and lipids.
Golgi Apparatus
Modifies, sorts and packages substances.
Lysosomes
Contain enzymes that break down waste and unwanted materials.
Small Fact
Plant cells also have mitochondria and ribosomes.
